# Milestone 19 — Onboarding and final polish

Milestone 19 makes the full game understandable without development context.

- A first-run Field Guide explains recruiting, automatic merges, positioning, traits, lives, and the core keyboard controls. Dismissal is remembered locally; **How to play** reopens it at any time.
- A compact Combat Effects reference defines Stunned, Chilled, Vulnerable, Weakened, Silenced, Guarded, Rallied, and Evasive without covering the arena.
- The selected-animal panel combines its role, traits, ability, selling action, and keyboard placement grid in one place.
- Local synthesized ability cues now distinguish heavy control, defensive/healing, burst, debuff, and rally effects. Sound remains optional and never controls simulation.
- The run-complete panel adds compact performance details and a direct new-run action.

The visual direction stays within the established compact game interface. No saved run, account, telemetry, remote audio, or permanent progression is introduced.
